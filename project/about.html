About NaijaForage

NaijaForage helps people safely identify and learn about wild edible plants found across Nigeria's forests, savannas, and wetlands. The site provides plant profile information, an interactive feature-based identification tool, and safety guidance, so curious foragers and home cooks can recognize local wild plants with confidence.

Safety First

Never eat any wild plant you cannot identify with full confidence. Several edible plants listed here have toxic or unpleasant-tasting look-alikes — always cross-check with an experienced forager or local source before eating anything foraged in the wild.

How the Identification Tool Works

The identification tool filters a growing list of plants by region and type. Your search count is saved to this device's browser so the tool can greet you with how many times you've used it.
