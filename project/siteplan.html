2. Site Name

NaijaForage

The name pairs "Naija," the common short form for Nigeria, with "Forage," the core activity the site supports. It signals both the regional focus and the purpose at a glance, and reads clearly to both English-speaking and Pidgin-speaking visitors.

Optional domain availability: naijaforage.com

3. Site Purpose

NaijaForage helps people safely identify and learn about wild edible plants found across Nigeria's forests, savannas, and wetlands. The site provides plant profile pages with identification details and look-alike warnings, an interactive feature-based identification tool, and a seasonal availability guide, so that curious foragers and home cooks can recognize local wild plants with confidence.

4. Scenarios

Scenario 1: "I saw a leafy plant growing wild near my compound in Akwa-Ibom. It looks a bit like bitterleaf, but I'm not sure — how do I check what it actually is before I try cooking with it?"

Scenario 2: "I'm visiting a market in the Southwest and I keep seeing Agbalumo everywhere. What time of year is it actually in season, and are there other wild fruits I should look out for right now?"

7. Typography

Two fonts are used across the site:

Fraunces — used for headings

Public Sans — used for body text, captions, and navigation. Fraunces brings an organic, slightly hand-cut character that suits a plant-identification site, while Public Sans keeps long descriptive passages easy to read at small sizes.

8. Typography Used on Document

This document applies both selected fonts: all headings above are set in Fraunces, and all body paragraphs, lists, and captions are set in Public Sans.
